History of the Organization

Kontained Living is a Johannesburg based leader in premium corporate transport and logistics solutions for corporates. We were founded in 2020 and deliver executive mobility and heavy-duty freight services tailored to the operational demands of modern enterprises. With a commitment to reliability, efficiency and professionalism, we ensure your personnel, clients, and assets move secure and on schedule.

Mission

To provide safe, affordable, and dependable transport services while delivering excellent customer service and maintaining high standards of professionalism.

Vision

To become one of South Africa's leading shuttle and transport companies through quality service, customer satisfaction, and continuous growth.

Uncomproming Safety & Compliance

We maintain the highest health and safety standards through fully compliant operations, professionally vetted and defensive-driving certified drivers, and regular medical fitness assessments to ensure maximum safety and risk mitigation for your business.
